One common main serves the whole scheme. Cloud 9 Reservoir is a fixed-head boundary at TWL; the pump injects a set flow at CH 6000. Continuity alone fixes the flow in each reach, so raising pump flow first reverses the reach below Snow Town and then works its way back up the main. Head always falls in whichever direction the water is actually travelling.
Residual head is measured above finished surface level. Pressure conversion at 9.81 kPa per metre of head. Pump head required is the grade line the pump must hold at CH 6000, above the plant water level; shaft power assumes 75 % combined pump and motor efficiency.
